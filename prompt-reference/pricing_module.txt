CAR RENTAL BACKEND — PRICING MODULE REFERENCE (M2.5 - M2.11)
=============================================================
Everything here is configuration the owner edits in the dashboard - never
hardcode prices, thresholds or taxes. Money is integer pesewas.
Runnable examples: http/pricing.http. Permissions: pricing.view (read),
pricing.manage (write), extras.manage (extras).

Status: M2.5 - M2.11 DONE - Phase 2 pricing complete (rates, price rules, extras,
protection plans, delivery zones, fees, taxes, quote engine).


M2.5 RATES (rate_plans)
-----------------------
One plan = the price of a category (or one car) for a drive type over a date range.
category_id      required
car_id           optional - a car's own plan overrides its category's plan
drive_type       self_drive | chauffeur
daily_amount     price per day (required, > 0)
weekly_amount    price for 7 days, used from settings pricing.weekly_threshold_days
                 (default 7); null = daily price applies
monthly_amount   price for 30 days, used from pricing.monthly_threshold_days (default
                 28); null = weekly (or daily) applies
mileage_mode     default (business setting rental.mileage_policy +
                 rental.daily_km_allowance) | unlimited | limited
included_km_per_day, excess_km_amount   limited only (both required then)
Chauffeur only (cleared on self-drive plans):
  out_of_town_daily_amount   per day when the trip leaves town (null = daily)
  driver_overnight_amount    allowance per night away
  driver_hours_per_day       driver's working day, then
  driver_overtime_hour_amount per extra hour
  fuel_included              chauffeur rentals in Ghana are usually fuel-excluded
effective_from / effective_to   optional dates (inclusive), for scheduled price changes
active           deactivate instead of deleting (bookings will reference plans)

Rules
- At most ONE active plan per (category, car-or-none, drive type) on any date:
  Postgres exclusion constraint rate_plans_no_overlap -> 409 E_RATE_OVERLAP.
  To change prices from a date: give the old plan an end date, add the new one.
- A car override must use the car's own category (422).
- Chauffeur-only categories (self_drive_allowed=false) can't have self-drive plans (422).

RateService.resolve({ categoryId, carId?, driveType, on }) -> plan | null
  Car override first, then the category plan, active and covering `on`.
  The quote engine (M2.11) calls it per rental day.

Endpoints
GET  /api/v1/admin/rate-plans       pricing.view  filters: categoryId, carId, driveType,
                                                  active, scope (category | car), page, limit
                                     response also has `context`: weeklyThresholdDays,
                                     monthlyThresholdDays, mileagePolicy, dailyKmAllowance
                                     (the settings rates depend on, for the rates page)
GET  /api/v1/admin/rate-plans/:id   pricing.view
POST /api/v1/admin/rate-plans       pricing.manage
PUT  /api/v1/admin/rate-plans/:id   pricing.manage (full payload)
No DELETE - set active=false.

Audit: rate_plan.created, rate_plan.updated


M2.6 PRICE RULES (pricing_rules) - seasons, holidays, weekends
---------------------------------------------------------------
Adjust the rate's day price on matching days. Applied PER CALENDAR DAY of a rental.
name, type          season | holiday | weekend
date_from/date_to   season & holiday: required from (to = from for a one-day holiday);
                    weekend: optional period limit
recurs_yearly       season/holiday only: match month-day every year (Christmas,
                    Independence Day); ranges may wrap the year end (20 Dec - 5 Jan);
                    must be shorter than a year. Moving holidays (Easter) = one-off rules.
weekdays            weekend only: ISO 1 (Mon) - 7 (Sun), e.g. [5,6,7]
adjustment_type     percent (value in basis points: 2500 = +25%, -1000 = -10%;
                    -100% .. +500%) | fixed (pesewas per day, signed). Never 0.
applies_to          all | categories (category_ids) | cars (car_ids)
min_rental_days     the quote warns when the rental is shorter (e.g. 3 days at Christmas)
priority            0-100; among matching non-stackable rules the highest wins
                    (ties: holiday > season > weekend, then newest)
stackable           added on top of the winner instead of competing
block_online_booking  public site can't book those dates (staff still can) - Phase 7
active, notes

PricingRuleService (pure functions; the quote engine loads activeRules() once):
  coversDay(rule, day), appliesTo(rule, {categoryId, carId}),
  adjustDay(base, day, rules, target) -> { amount, applied[{id,name,change,...}] }
  Every change is worked out on the BASE day price (no compounding); day >= 0.

Endpoints
GET    /api/v1/admin/pricing-rules       pricing.view  filters: search, type, active
GET    /api/v1/admin/pricing-rules/:id   pricing.view
POST   /api/v1/admin/pricing-rules       pricing.manage
PUT    /api/v1/admin/pricing-rules/:id   pricing.manage (full payload)
DELETE /api/v1/admin/pricing-rules/:id   pricing.manage (bookings copy the rule name, so
                                         deleting a rule never changes past prices)
Responses include `categories` / `cars` [{id, label}] for display.
Audit: pricing_rule.created / updated / deleted


M2.7 - M2.9 CATALOG: extras, protection plans, delivery zones, fees
--------------------------------------------------------------------
All four: name, description, sort_order, active (deactivate - never delete;
bookings will reference them), created_by, audited as <subject>.created/updated.
List endpoints filter by search (name) and active; ordered active first, sort order, name.

extras            charge_type per_day | per_rental, amount, max_quantity (per booking,
                  1-20), stock (null = unlimited; checked against bookings in Phase 3),
                  taxable (exempt items untick it - M2.10), available_online
protection_plans  per_day_amount, excess_amount (most the customer pays for damage -
                  printed on the agreement, caps damage charges at check-in),
                  deposit_amount_override (null = category deposit), is_default (ONE
                  default: partial unique index; setting it clears the others; an
                  inactive plan is never the default), taxable, available_online
delivery_zones    delivery_amount, collection_amount, after_hours_amount,
                  available_online. Office pickup = no zone = free.
fees              code (unique slug, fixed after creation), unit flat | per_hour |
                  per_day | per_litre | per_km | percent (basis points, max 100%),
                  amount, taxable, is_system. Standard codes used by check-in (Phase 4):
                  late_return_hour, late_return_day, fuel_per_litre, refuel_service,
                  cleaning_standard, cleaning_heavy, smoking, lost_key, lost_document,
                  excess_km, admin_fee_fines.

Seeder database/seeders/pricing_catalog_seeder.ts (run by node ace db:seed): adds
missing standard fees by code, and example extras / plans / zones only into EMPTY
tables - all INACTIVE for the owner to review. Never changes existing rows.

Endpoints (GET pricing.view; writes pricing.manage, extras: extras.manage)
GET/POST /api/v1/admin/extras            PUT /api/v1/admin/extras/:id
GET/POST /api/v1/admin/protection-plans  PUT /api/v1/admin/protection-plans/:id
GET/POST /api/v1/admin/delivery-zones    PUT /api/v1/admin/delivery-zones/:id
GET/POST /api/v1/admin/fees              PUT /api/v1/admin/fees/:id (code ignored)


M2.10 TAXES (taxes) + business.vat_registered
-----------------------------------------------------
Decided with the owner: taxes attach to the TYPE OF CHARGE, never to cars.
Whether tax is charged at all = the business profile's vat_registered (Settings > Business,
with the VAT number) - ONE source of truth. While false, no tax is added anywhere.
No taxes are seeded: Ghana's structure changed in 2025-26; the accountant supplies them.

name, description     e.g. VAT, NHIL, GETFund levy
rate_bp               basis points, 1 .. 10000 (1500 = 15%)
calculation           on_net (on the charge) | on_net_plus_levies (on the charge plus
                      the on_net taxes of that line - for a tax charged on levies)
applies_to            rental | extras | protection | delivery | fees (>= 1, stored in
                      that order)
effective_from/to     optional; rate changes = end the old row, add a new one, so past
                      invoices stay reproducible
sort_order            order on receipts; active (deactivate, never delete)

TaxService
  calculate(lines, taxes) - pure. Per line: skip taxable=false and 0; on_net taxes on
    the amount, then compound taxes on amount + that line's levies. Rounded to the
    pesewa PER LINE, then summed. Discounts are negative lines (lower the base).
    Taxes with nothing to tax are omitted. Returns { lines[{taxId,name,rateBp,
    calculation,base,amount}], total }.
  activeOn(day) - active taxes covering the day, in receipt order.
  isVatRegistered() - business.vat_registered.
  forLines(lines, day) - checks VAT registration, loads activeOn(day), calculates.
  Deposits are never passed in - never taxed, never revenue.

Endpoints
GET  /api/v1/admin/taxes       pricing.view (search, active); response `context`:
                               { vatRegistered } (from the business profile)
POST /api/v1/admin/taxes       settings.manage
PUT  /api/v1/admin/taxes/:id   settings.manage
Registration itself: PUT /api/v1/admin/business { vatRegistered, vatNumber } (settings.manage)
Audit: tax.created / tax.updated (+ business.updated for registration)


M2.11 QUOTE ENGINE - PricingService.quote(input) (app/services/pricing/pricing_service.ts)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE single price calculation: staff quotes now; bookings, changes, check-in and the public
site later. Input: carId, pickupAt, returnAt (DateTime, business time zone), driveType,
outOfTown?, driverNightsAway?, extras[{extraId, quantity}], protectionPlanId (undefined =
default plan, null = none), deliveryZoneId?, collectionZoneId?.

Steps
1. Car must exist, not archived, status active; self-drive needs category.self_drive_allowed.
2. rentalDays = max(1, ceil((minutes - booking.return_grace_minutes) / 1440)).
3. Per calendar day from the pickup date: plan = car override else category plan covering
   that day (planOn); base = out-of-town price (chauffeur + outOfTown) | monthly/30 (days >=
   pricing.monthly_threshold_days) | weekly/7 (>= weekly threshold) | daily; then
   PricingRuleService.adjustDay. Any day without a plan -> QuoteError (lists the dates).
4. Lines: rental (sum of days), driver overnight allowance x nights, extras (per day x days
   or per rental, x quantity <= max_quantity), protection (per day x days), delivery /
   collection zone fees + the zone's after-hours fee when the time is outside opening
   hours; office handover outside hours -> booking.after_hours_fee (0 = warning instead).
5. Taxes: TaxService.forLines - rental per day, other lines per line, by charge type
   (driver allowance = rental, after hours = fees), on the pickup date.
6. total = subtotal + taxes. deposit = plan.deposit_amount_override ?? category deposit
   (never taxed, not in total). reservationFee when payment.allow_pay_at_pickup
   (percent of total, or fixed capped at total).
Output also: days[{date, base, adjustments[{name, change}], amount}], tier, protection
{name, excessAmount}, mileage {unlimited, includedKm, excessKmAmount} (rate mode, else
settings; excess from the rate or the excess_km fee), chauffeur terms, vatRegistered,
displayTaxInclusive, warnings[{code, message}].

QuoteError (-> 422 with field): car missing/archived/not active, chauffeur-only category,
return <= pickup, no rate for some days, inactive/over-quantity extra, inactive plan/zone.
Warnings (quote still returned): unavailable (allocation overlap), min/max rental days,
lead time / past pickup, rule_min_days:<ruleId>, pickup/return_after_hours (no fee),
pickup/return_closure (closed weekday or closure date).
Opening hours: weekday 0 = Sunday..6; no hours set = open.

Endpoint: POST /api/v1/admin/quotes (pricing.view for now; bookings.create in Phase 3).
Body times are ISO strings, e.g. "2026-12-20T09:00", read in the business time zone.
Tests: tests/functional/quotes.spec.ts (grace, tiers, season crossing, weekends, extras,
protection/deposit, delivery & after hours, closures, taxes, chauffeur, errors).
